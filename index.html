A Few Helpful Details.

Can I buy or rent an outfit?+

Wabi Sabi offers sales and rentals. Options depend on the piece. Ask the boutique to confirm availability and pricing for your occasion.

How do I arrange a rental?+

Share your event date and preferred look. Confirm the fit, rental charge, deposit, pickup and return dates, and any additional charges before booking.

Are these photos of actual shop stock?+

These AI-created editorial images show styling inspiration. Actual outfits, colours, sizes, prices, and availability should be confirmed at the boutique.
